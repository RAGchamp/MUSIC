What does the Piano Notes Player do?

It plays a song's notes (a notes file from REFERENCE-AUDIO-FILES) on a piano sound and shows two hands playing them on the keyboard: the right hand (orange) and the left hand (blue) move along the keys and press them with numbered fingers (1 = thumb … 5 = little finger). Under the keyboard the notes scroll by in staff notation, so you can see what is played and how. The hands, the left/right split and the fingering are worked out from the notes by the page (a sensible guess, not the arranger's fingering). No AI runs in this page.
