What does the Piano page do?

It captures notes with their exact times in the recording. Play the keys yourself with your laptop keyboard (or the mouse), optionally while the original WAV plays, even slowed down, or let the page find the notes in a WAV file and press the keys as it plays. Every key stroke is written in the box below as a song-library.js note line (time in seconds, note, beats), ready to be checked with the Notes Tester. No AI runs in this page.

BPM

Each line is 'time notes beats': the time is the second in the WAV where the key went down, the beats are how long it was held (seconds × tempo ÷ 60, rounded to ¼). Keys pressed within 0.05 s of each other become one chord line (highest note first). Two hands: press 📥 Start from the song's lines (or record the tune first), keep "Add to the lines already in the box" ticked and record the other hand: each key stroke within the join distance of a line is added to that line's notes (its time, beats and main note stay as they are); other strokes become lines of their own. Paste the lines into Claude Code to put them in song-library.js, then check them with the Notes Tester.
