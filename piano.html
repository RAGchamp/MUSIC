How does the RAG Champ Piano app work?

It plays a song's notes on a real piano sound and shows two hands playing them: the right hand (orange) and the left hand (blue) slide along the keys and press them with numbered fingers (1 = thumb … 5 = little finger). Below, the score scrolls in step, with the notes being played in yellow. Slow it down or loop a few bars to learn any piece. Try it with Für Elise or the Bollywood song Pehla Nasha.
