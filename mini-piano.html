Play a tune by hand, then Copy to table: your keys and the time between your taps go into the Automated table so you can replay or edit them. On a laptop, keys A–K are the white keys and W E T Y U the black ones.

#Key to pressWait (ms)
Wait = time from this key to the next one (50–5000 ms). The last row's wait is how long the last note rings. Tap a row's #, then tap piano keys to fill rows one after another.

No sound on an iPhone? Turn off silent mode (the switch on the side).
